CQI360Program Evaluation Plan Builder

Build a Program Evaluation Plan

An 11-step model for defining, measuring, and iterating on the evaluation of any program, project, or partnership — from objectives through a final plan.

Purpose

This tool walks you, step by step, through building a complete evaluation plan for your own program or project. Work through each step in order, entering your own content as you go. There's no single right answer — the goal is to leave with a plan you can act on, not just a filled-in form. Your progress saves automatically, so you can complete it in one sitting or come back to it later.
